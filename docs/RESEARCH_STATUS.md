# Research status and limits

The public companion preserves the implementation and its original tests. It is not a new
experiment, independent review or proof of scientific novelty. Detailed historical studies stay
in the private research archive identified in [export provenance](PUBLIC_EXPORT.md).

Historical observations from that archive:

- The early real-model pilot's verified arm abstained on every case. Useful triage efficacy was
  not established.
- The frozen v0.3 real-model study recorded 1,280 arm/model/case observations. Its incident-recall
  noninferiority target was not established for either evaluated model. Failures remained in the
  denominator; these results have not been replaced by later software checks.
- Later source-pinned approval context reduced unexplained benign flags from 4/8 to 0/8 on new
  authored controls while retaining 12/12 positive flags. Original controls without that context
  retained their 4/16 benign flags. These are constructed software controls, not population FPR.
- Two inspected public development windows demonstrated source interoperability. They lacked
  independent incident labels and did not establish real-world detection efficacy.

The public demo and mock benchmark test software behavior. They do not evaluate an LLM, invent
incident intent, replace independent annotation or establish comparative research superiority.
Separately recomputed predicates certify only the constrained statements they check over admitted
records. Hashes bind bytes under the custody assumptions; they do not certify truthful telemetry.

Outstanding research needs include independent eligible cases, documented source permissions,
blinded human annotations, an adjudication process, a predeclared prospective study, calibrated
decision criteria and external security review. Review forms remain blank until real reviewers
submit their judgments. No human identity or independent review is inferred from a filled schema.

Original code and synthetic fixtures are MIT. External data are not redistributed here. See the
[historical license audit](LICENSE_AUDIT.md) for dependency and dataset caveats; its dated advisory
scan is not a current universal vulnerability clearance.
